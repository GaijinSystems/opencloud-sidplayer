C64 ROM images (kernal.bin, basic.bin, chargen.bin)
  The Commodore 64 KERNAL, BASIC and character ROMs, copyright Commodore
  Business Machines and its successors in title. They are not covered by
  this app's licence. They are included so that RSID and BASIC tunes play;
  remove them, or build with scripts/package.sh --no-roms, where that is
  not a redistribution you are able to make. Without them PSID tunes still play.
