# Demo collection

The ten tunes of the demo site at https://sidplayer.gaijin.systems, laid
out as a user's `SID Library` folder: nine from the High Voltage SID
Collection, one from the Compute's Gazette SID Collection, all ten starred in
this order, and the collection marked online.

They are not part of the app: not on `main`, and in no build of the app or
its release tarball. `_demo/build.sh` copies the tunes into the site, which
plays them from there, and builds the two files under `.sidplayer/` into the
page itself.

The HVSC tunes are exactly the files of HVSC release 85; each matches its MD5
in that release's `Songlengths.md5`, which is also what the favourites record.
The tunes belong to their composers, and are here as the collections
distribute them.
